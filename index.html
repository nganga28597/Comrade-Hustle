QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How do I find work on ComradeHustle?

Create an account as a freelancer, browse available campus jobs, and contact a hirer through the job listing.

How do I post a task?

Create an account as a hirer, then choose “Post a Task” from your profile. Add the task details, budget, location, and deadline so students know what to expect.

Are payments handled by ComradeHustle?

Not in this demo. Agree on payment details directly with the other person and verify them before starting work. Do not share sensitive account details in chat.

How can I contact someone about a job?

Open the job listing and use its contact option. You need to be logged in as a freelancer to contact a hirer.

Can I trust every job listing?

Listings are provided by users. Confirm the task, expectations, and payment with the hirer, and avoid offers that ask for money or sensitive personal information.

How it Works

ComradeHustle helps students find flexible work and helps campus hirers get things done.

01

For students

  1. Create an account as a freelancer.
  2. Browse campus jobs and check the requirements, pay, and deadline.
  3. Contact the hirer, agree on the details, and complete the task.
02

For hirers

  1. Create an account as a hirer.
  2. Post a clear task with its budget, location, and deadline.
  3. Review interested students, agree on the work, and confirm completion.

ComradeHustle is currently a demo: it does not process payments or guarantee listings.
